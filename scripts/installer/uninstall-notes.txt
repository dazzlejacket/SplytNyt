SplytNyt - uninstall notes
==========================

SplytNyt has been removed from this computer.

Your own data has been left in place:

    %USERPROFILE%\.splytnyt

That folder holds:

  settings.json          your settings
  projects / exports     anything SplytNyt saved for you
  models/                the optional YAMNet and Whisper models, if you
                         downloaded them (about 500 MB in total)
  optional_engines/      the optional ShazamIO (Experimental) engine, if you
                         installed it
  cache/                 cached online metadata lookups

Nothing in that folder is needed to run SplytNyt again, and nothing in it is
sent anywhere. The uninstaller offers to delete it for you if you want it gone;
if you chose to keep it, you can delete the folder yourself at any time.

Your own audio recordings were never touched. SplytNyt never writes over a
source recording, and it never uploads one.
